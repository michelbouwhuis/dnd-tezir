"First Concubine," Adonides is a powerful erinyes who serves [[Glasya]] faithfully.

Adonides is lui en laf. Hij zal de helden niet zomaar aanvallen, maar liever anderen inschakelen. Tevens zal hij pas vechten als hij denkt dat hij kan winnen.

Hij kreeg van Glasya de opdracht om [[Mirell]] uit te schakelen. Hiervoor heeft hij [[Jelara Swiftwind]] om de tuin geleid door haar beelden uit een alternatief universum te laten zien. Jelara zag Miral als Asmodora bijna alle macht in de hellen naar zich toe trekken. 

[[Asmodeus]] was hier verantwoordelijk voor, want hij zag veel potentie in die versie van Mirell.

Adonides can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

- **At will:** _Thaumaturgy, Minor Illusion, Prestidigitation_
- **3/day each:** _Charm Person, Suggestion, Invisibility_
- **1/day each:** _Greater Invisibility, Dominate Person_

---
### **Bardic Spellcasting (Level 13 Bard, College of Swords)**

Adonides is a 13th-level Bard. His spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 19, +11 to hit). He has the following spells prepared:

- **Cantrips:** _Vicious Mockery, Mage Hand, Message, Blade Ward_
- **1st level (4 slots):** _Disguise Self, Heroism, Feather Fall, Cure Wounds_
- **2nd level (3 slots):** _Mirror Image, Hold Person_
- **3rd level (3 slots):** _Hypnotic Pattern, Counterspell_
- **4th level (3 slots):** _Greater Invisibility, Polymorph_
- **5th level (2 slots):** _Modify Memory, Hold Monster_
- **6th level (1 slot):** _Otto’s Irresistible Dance_

---

### **Legendary Courtesan** _(3/Day)_

If Adonides fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

---

### **Seducer’s Grace** _(Recharge 5-6)_

As a bonus action, Adonides can choose one creature within 30 feet that can hear and see him. The target must make a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or be Charmed for 1 minute. While Charmed, the target has disadvantage on saving throws against Adonides' spells. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns.

---

### **Actions**

#### **Multiattack.**

Adonides makes two Flourishing Blade attacks or one Flourishing Blade attack and casts a cantrip.

#### **Flourishing Blade.** _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target.

**Hit:** 9 (1d8+4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage. On a hit, Adonides can use one of the following Flourishes:

- **Defensive Flourish:** Add 1d10 to AC until the start of his next turn.
- **Slashing Flourish:** Deal an extra 1d10 slashing damage to another creature within 5 ft.
- **Mobile Flourish:** Push the target 10 feet away and move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

#### **Silver-Tongued Gambit (Recharge 5-6).**

Adonides targets a creature within 30 feet that can hear him. The creature must make a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or become **Stunned** until the start of its next turn as they are overwhelmed by his presence.

---

### **Bonus Actions**

#### **Mantle of Majesty (1/Day).**

For 1 minute, Adonides can cast _Command_ as a bonus action on each of his turns without expending a spell slot.

#### **Cunning Step.**

Adonides takes the Disengage or Dash action.

---

### **Reactions**

#### **Dueling Wit.**

When a creature targets Adonides with an attack, he can impose disadvantage if the creature is Charmed or Frightened.

---

### **Lair Actions (If in His Palace or Domain)**

On initiative count 20 (losing ties), Adonides can use one of the following effects:

- **Alluring Mirage:** He casts _Mirror Image_ without expending a spell slot.
- **Velvet Chains:** One creature he can see within 30 feet must make a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw or be Restrained for 1 minute. The target repeats the save at the end of each of its turns.
- **The Eyes of the Court:** Adonides momentarily sees through the eyes of every person in his lair, granting him blindsight for 1 minute.

---

### **Tactics & Personality**

Adonides is a master manipulator, preferring to weaken his foes before fighting. He opens with _Hypnotic Pattern_ or _Hold Monster_ to neutralize threats, then follows up with his _Flourishing Blade_. He uses _Greater Invisibility_ and _Mirror Image_ to stay elusive. If forced into melee, he relies on his high Charisma to outmaneuver opponents, striking with precision while maintaining his unshakable composure.
