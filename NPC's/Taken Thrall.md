Monster

Medium aberration, chaotic evil

---
Armor Class 12  
Hit Points 30 (7d8 + 14)  
Speed 30 ft.

---

STR 12 (+1) DEX 14 (+2) CON 14 (+2) INT 5 (-3) WIS 10 (+0) CHA 6 (-2)

---

Skills Stealth +4  
Senses darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 10

Damage Resistances acid, fire, lightning, thunder; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks

Damage Immunities necrotic, poison

Condition Immunities exhaustion, frightened, grappled, paralyzed, petrified, poisoned

Languages Understands Abyssal but can't speak  
Challenge 2 (450 XP)

---

**Taken Essence**. Whenever the Taken Thrall is reduced to 0 hit points, it explodes. Each creature within 5 feet of it must make a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw, taking 10 (3d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.  
  
**Incorporeal Movement**. The Taken Thrall can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. It takes 5 (1d10) force damage if it ends its turn inside an object.

**Mitosis**. When the Taken Thrall starts his turn with 1-5 hit points remaining. The Taken Thrall makes a copy of itself with full health. Cannot attack directly after  Mitosis. (The copy cannot use Mitosis.)

**Erratic movement**. The Taken Thrall never moves in a straight line.

---

Actions: 2 per turn

Claw. Melee Weapon Attack: +7 `dice:1d20+7` to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 8 (1d8 + 4 `dice:1d8+4`) slashing damage plus 2 (1d6 `dice:1d6`) cold damage.

Teleport: magically teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space it can see.
