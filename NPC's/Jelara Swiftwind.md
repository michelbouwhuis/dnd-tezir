## **Features & Traits**

### **Fey Ancestry:**

Advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and cannot be put to sleep magically.

### **Jack of All Trades:**

Adds half proficiency bonus (+2) to ability checks that don't already have proficiency.

### **Song of Rest (d8):**

If Jelara or her allies regain HP using Hit Dice during a short rest, they gain an extra **1d8 HP**.

### **Bardic Inspiration (d8, 5 per long rest):**

As a bonus action, Jelara can inspire an ally within 60 feet, granting them a d8 to add to an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw within 10 minutes.

### **Expertise:**

Jelara doubles her proficiency bonus for Arcana and Performance.

### **Cutting Words:**

When a creature within 60 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or damage roll, Jelara can use her reaction to subtract a **d8** from the roll.

### **Magical Secrets (Lore Bard 6th level):**

Jelara has learned two spells from any class:

- **Counterspell (3rd level, Wizard)**
- **Spirit Guardians (3rd level, Cleric)**
