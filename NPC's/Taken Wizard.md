Spellcasting. The Taken Wizard is a 7th-level spellcaster. Its spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). The Taken Wizard has the following wizard spells prepared:

Cantrips (at will): Eldritch Blast, Mage Hand, Minor Illusion
* 1st level (4 slots): Mage Armor, Magic Missile, Shield
* 2nd level (3 slots): Darkness, Mirror Image
* 3rd level (3 slots): Counterspell, Lightning Bolt
* 4th level (1 slot): Blight

Taken Essence. Whenever the Taken Wizard is reduced to 0 hit points, it explodes. Each creature within 15 feet of it must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 9 (3d6) force damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.
