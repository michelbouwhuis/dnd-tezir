Gulrot, Unclean is een Hive Ogre Wizard. 

**Medium Fiend (Hive), Chaotic Evil**  
**Armor Class** 18 (Natural Armor)  
**Hit Points** 225 (18d8 + 126)  
**Speed** 30 ft., Fly 40 ft. (Hover)

#### **Abilities**

**STR** 14 (+2) | **DEX** 16 (+3) | **CON** 24 (+7) | **INT** 18 (+4) | **WIS** 14 (+2) | **CHA** 22 (+6)

**Saving Throws** CON +11, INT +8, CHA +10  
**Damage Resistances** Necrotic, Psychic, Cold  
**Damage Immunities** Poison  
**Condition Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned  
**Senses** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 14  
**Languages** Deep Speech, Abyssal, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Proficiency = 4**

### **Actions (3 per round)**

**0. Cast one spell (Costs 1 Action). 1 target per spell.

**1. Necrotic Blast (Costs 1 Action, 1 per speler per beurt)** 
Gulrot targets one creature within 60 feet it can see. The target makes a **DC 19 Dexterity** save, taking **3d10 `dice:3d10` necrotic damage** on a fail or half on a success. Gulrot can also play this as a reaction on seeing one of the players. Remeber: only 1 reaction per turn.

**2. Teleport (Costs 2 Actions):**  
Gulrot magically teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space he can see.

**3. Spawn Thrall (Costs 3 Actions):**  
Gulrot summons 2  (`dice:1d3`) taken minions. Then for each, roll 1d3: `dice:1d3` 1 = [[Taken Thrall | Thrall]], 2 = [[Taken Wizard | Wizard]] and 3 = [[Taken Knight | Knight]], which appears in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet of him. It acts immediately after his turn. (Check hoe moeilijk het wordt. Indien te moeilijk alleen Spawn als hij geen damage heeft gehad)

**4. Bile explosion (Costs 2 Actions)
In a 20 foot region, all terrain becomes diffucult terrain (movement speed is halved, for flying and walking).

### **Spellcasting (Warlock 12, Spell Save DC 19)**

Gulrot can cast the following Warlock spells:

**At-will:** Eldritch Blast with 3 beams. Hit: `dice:1d20+6+4` 10 `dice:1d10+6` force damage per hit.
**2/day each:** Darkness, Counterspell, [Hunger of Hadar](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2339-hunger-of-hadar) Fear  
**1/day each: **[Finger of Death](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2101-finger-of-death)

### **Tactics & Fight Phases** (optional)

1. **Phase 1:** Gulrot fights at range, using **Eldritch Blast**, **Darkness**, and **Necrotic Blast** to harass players. Thralls swarm the party.
2. **Phase 2 (75% HP):** He uses **Shadow Wrath**, teleports frequently, and summons Acolytes. He casts **Hunger of Hadar** to control the battlefield.
3. **Phase 3 (50% HP):** Gulrot becomes more aggressive, using **Finger of Death**.
4. **Final Stand (25% HP):**Spamming **Legendary Actions**.
