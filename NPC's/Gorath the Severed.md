### Goliath Wizard 10 – Bladesinger**

**AC:** 18 (Bladesong + Mage Armor)  
**HP:** 85 (10d6 + 30)  
**Speed:** 40 ft (Bladesong active)  
**STR 18 | DEX 14 | CON 16 | INT 20 | WIS 12 | CHA 10**  
**Saving Throws:** INT +9, DEX +6  
**Skills:** Acrobatics +6 (Bladesong Advantage), Arcana +9, Athletics +8  
**Resistances:** Cold, Advantage on CON saves vs. exhaustion  

**Actions:** (Extra Attack)
- **Dagger Dance (Multiattack):** Melee Spell Attack +9, 10 ft reach, 2d8+5 slashing + 1d8 force.
- **Bladed Ward (Reaction):** Halveert melee schade 1/ronde.
- **Arcane Step (Bonus Action, 1/LR):** Teleporteert 15 ft zonder opportunity attacks.

**Spells:**
- **Haste** (3e) – Verhoogt snelheid, extra actie, +2 AC.
- **Shield** (1e, Reaction) – +5 AC tot begin volgende beurt.
- **Mirror Image** (2e) – Creëert 3 illusies die aanvallen mis kunnen laten gaan.

**Bladesong:**
- You gain a bonus to your AC equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1)
- Your walking speed increases by 10 feet.
- You have advantage on Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks.
- You gain a bonus to any Constitution saving throw you make to maintain your concentration on a spell. The bonus equals your Intelligence modifier (minimum of +1).
